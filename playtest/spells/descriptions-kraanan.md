# Kraanan spell descriptions, as the game shows them

Taken 2026-10-24 (22:45, after the server reloaded the level grids) from the real client protocol (BP_LOOK on each spell), for a character with Intellect 90 and a very high Mysticism (the same stats Scout has now), at 99% ability, standing in the Inn of Raza at server hour 12.
The damage lines include the stat bonus (up to +40%); a character with stats of 35 sees lower numbers. The light, hour, vigor and company lines move the spellpower, so compare against the spellpower each cast reports.
Spell levels moved when the tier grids changed: see tier-changes.md.

## create food

SCHOOL : Kraanan         LEVEL : 1 

The power of Kraanan is summoned to materialize various nourishing foods.  Requires nothing but free will to cast.  

Costs: 8 mana. Takes 0.3 seconds to cast. Your next spell can follow a second later.

Spellpower 86 (the most is 99)

   Your health: +10
   Kraanan's favour: +20

## create weapon

SCHOOL : Kraanan         LEVEL : 1 

Materializes a weapon.  Requires nothing but a violent will.  You may say the name of the weapon you want to create.  The choices are mace, short sword, hammer, axe, long sword, scimitar, mystic sword, and practice bow.  Naming a weapon ends the trance early and gives no chance to improve the spell; let the concentration finish to improve it.  

Costs: 13 mana, 1 vigor. Takes 3.2 seconds to cast. Your next spell can follow a second later.

Spellpower 86 (the most is 99)

## martyr's battleground

SCHOOL : Kraanan         LEVEL : 6 

Calls the gaze of Kraanan down upon the battle.  While it lasts, the caster's allies in the area, their companions and their faction's soldiers among them, strike truer and harder, and every death here renews them; the fall of one of their own renews them twice over.  Foes get nothing from it.  Requires elderberry and mushroom to cast.  

At your spellpower: Allies gain 135 to hit and 3 damage a blow (monsters 27% more offense), and heal 6 health at each death here (12 at an ally's), for 200 seconds.
Costs: 8 mana, 1 vigor, 1 mushroom, 1 elderberry. Your next spell can follow a second later.
