# Frular's maturity mail says allies enter a hall "as if it were their own"

The guild-is-now-mature mail (guild.kod guild_mature_mail) has an ALLIES paragraph that contradicts the rules built in phase 1, and its own VISITORS paragraph above it. Allies get in only at the guild's "Sir" rank and above, and only when the hall is private.

## Steps to reproduce

Read kod/object/passive/guild.kod lines ~270-312, or mature a guild (set piMature to 0 on the Guild) and read Frular's second mail. Paragraphs: "VISITORS: ... Only your own members, and members of allied guilds of rank '%q' and above, may open its outer door." and then "ALLIES: If you declare a guild to be an ally, then they may enter your guild hall as if it were their own."

## Expected

One consistent rule. ADR 0022 decision 7: allied guilds at Member (Sir) and up; a recruit of an allied guild can't enter a private hall (GuildHall.CanEnter checks the rank).

## Actual

The two paragraphs disagree. I also saw (in the guild_rent texts, guild.kod ~195-198 and gcreator.kod ~53-59) that a guild that doesn't pay rent is still told it "will be disbanded", whereas decision 4 says an inactive guild loses its hall, not itself; that one may be intended (rent default is separate from inactivity), but worth confirming. Checked in the game: Recruit and Sir behaviour for the guild's own members, the stranger's refusal and the public toggle all match the first paragraph.
