Welcome to Meridian Shards!

This shard is still being tested, so expect the odd restart and a few rough edges. The world is saved every half hour.

New to Meridian? Marcus, who keeps the Inn of Raza where you arrive, has your first quest: look for the gold ! over his head. Right-click things to see what you can do with them, and press O for the settings.

Found a problem? Let us know, and thank you for playing.
