# Playtest notes, 2026-10-25

- 0012 (Outskirts of Raza [razaforest.roo, RID 330]): Riija balance (Scout, SP 52-63, mace gives +5 general spellpower to Riija!). Disguise bonus = (spellpower at cast + 9)/10, max 10: Anonymity +6 (cast at 52, 14 mana, lasts exactly 6:00), Shadow Form +6 (3:51), Illusionary Form +6 (1:46, random half to full of 90+2*SP s), Morph +7 (cast at 63; cow, max health 100->68, gear unequipped, 20 mana, 10 s cast, lasted ~7:45); bonus does not stack (strongest only), so about +11% spellpower; attack spells do not break disguises. Best: Anonymity (cheapest, longest, keeps gear). Illusionary Wounds: 22 dmg at SP57 vs 24 at SP63 on the dummy (scales with SP), 11-14 on rat/orc (1/3 cap, never below 10 HP), picks a random element, aggro-less. Bewildered (+10%): Fireball baseline mean 14.6 hit/14.9 'takes' over 18 casts; after Vertigo 15.8/16.2 (9 casts, +8%), after Wounds 15.6-16.3/16.2 (9 casts, +9%). Vertigo durations: rat 21-22, spider 22, orc 19-21, skeleton 20, zombie 20, mummy 19, troll 21 (random 19-22, same on all). Forget 44 s, Dement 124 s, Seduce 44 s (an orc, followed and stopped attacking). Denial: 75 s of full health, then only ~23% of the healing kept (74 -> 100 -> 80). Illusionary Fire Wall: 3-21 per tick (avg ~10) about every 1.4 s on a stationary dummy = ~7 dps for 144 s, 14 mana: strongest damage per mana, way stronger than Fireball (15 per 7 mana per 2 s). Reflection: lasts ~10 min (1 mana / 8 s), killed a 60 HP orc in ~45 s alone, died to a Troll in one hit. Evil Twin: died in ~15 s, did ~15 damage to an orc. Apparition (baby spider/centipede at 1/4 health) did ~5-8 damage per minute: 'good damage' is wrong at SP 57. Transmogrify: orc at ~45% health became a cow with same wounds, no loot (unmodified orc dropped scimitar, leather armor, orc teeth, shillings). Elusion: destinations Marion, Raza, Cor Noth, East Jasper; say the name. Phase: 640 s of phase time shown at SP 59. Natural mana regen at rest: 58 -> 89 in about 2 min.
- 0052 (Outskirts of Raza [razaforest.roo, RID 330]): Jala round 1 numbers (spellpower 60-65 without instrument, noon, Outskirts RID 330). FOE SONGS tick every 5 s (code: duration 5000+sp ms, refreshed). Damage songs (Sacred, Profane, Disharmony): Random(1,3)+sp/25 = 3-5 per foe per pulse at sp 65 (observed 3-5 on every dummy line; rats/spiders die in ~5 pulses, a skeleton in 10 pulses; undead double = 6-10 per pulse). Mana: cast 4-5, then 3 mana per pulse (0.6/s), no vigor per tick; Warp Time 5/pulse (1/s, not in the description), Spellbane 8/pulse (1.6/s), Mana Convergence 9/pulse (1.8/s), Jig 10/pulse (2/s), Rejuvenate 1/pulse, Invigorate/Restorate/Mirth/Melancholy 0 after the cast. Songs run until you rest (stand->sit ends them: "Your discordant tune fades."), cast the same song again (toggle), or enter a no-combat room (Inn: "Your strangely mechanical song fades." on arrival). Moving, fighting, casting other spells do NOT stop them; several songs stack. Mace hit for comparison: 3-9. => A damage song is ~0.8 dps per foe at 0.6 mana/s: fine per target but the room-wide reach (see bug) makes it an AoE of everything alive: 6 dummies = 4.8 dps for 0.6 mana/s. Verdict: about right per target, too strong in groups because of the missing range. WARP TIME measured on an Orc: attack interval 2.5 s -> 3.45 s (+38%, code 125+sp/4 = +41%); 1 mana/s. CIVILITY on an Orc: mean hit 3.67 (n=12) -> 3.2 (n=11) (-13%; code says -23% at sp 65; orc max hit stayed 4). JIG (sp 23-28 at 26% ability, I had just bought it for 4000 at Themala): while it played an adjacent Orc made NO attack for 36 s (and I could not attack: "It wouldn't feel right to attack the training dummy while you two are dancing so well together!"); costs 2 mana/s; refused in the Inn with "You can't fight here." although its description is about parties. INVIGORATE: vigor +44 in 35 s (1.26/s) and goes above max (120/80, 150%) - and with high vigor health regen stood at 1 HP/s natural. RESTORATE: code +1 HP per 100000/(sp+1) ms = 1 per 1.5 s at sp 65 (0.66/s, 1/s at sp 99); my natural regen with vigor 120 was ~1 HP/s so the song is a +65% regen for 5 herbs + a diamond. MANA CONVERGENCE adds the song's whole spellpower to attack spells (cap 99): Fireball sp 72 -> 99, damage mean 14.0 (n=5, 11-18) -> 14.4 (n=6, 13-17): the damage formula scales only (50+sp/2)/99, so at high spellpower it is about +15% at best for 1.8 mana/s + a polished seraphym; it also has viAffectsEnemies=TRUE so it would boost enemy players' attack spells in hearing (not in the description). MIRTH vs Zombie (karma -45): mace mean 3.6 (n=8) -> 4.1 (n=13); code says +1..2 flat per hit, effect not clearly visible. INSTRUMENTS: Lute +20 and Necklace of Jala +20 (examine: "20 Jala instrument power"), both = capped 99; base health part is baseHealth/12 capped at 10 (original M59 rule; Scout 100 base = +8). CRYSTALIZE MANA with my 339-mana stock: result Mana 250/29 (the surge sets mana to 250 at most, so with more than 250 it REMOVED 89; max shown 29/89 for about a minute) cost 19 mana, 24 vigor, uncut seraphym + sapphire; real test needs low mana (round 2). DISTILL: cast, then say the spell name within the 30 s window ("minor heal") -> done in 2 s: "Over encumbered, you drop a potion of healing at your feet." and Restorate+Invigorate faded at that moment.
