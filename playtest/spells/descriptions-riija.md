# Riija spell descriptions, as the game shows them

Taken 2026-10-24 (22:45, after the server reloaded the level grids) from the real client protocol (BP_LOOK on each spell), for a character with Intellect 90 and a very high Mysticism (the same stats Scout has now), at 99% ability, standing in the Inn of Raza at server hour 12.
The damage lines include the stat bonus (up to +40%); a character with stats of 35 sees lower numbers. The light, hour, vigor and company lines move the spellpower, so compare against the spellpower each cast reports.
Spell levels moved when the tier grids changed: see tier-changes.md.

## anonymity

SCHOOL : Riija         LEVEL : 1 

Makes you indistinct, and unrecognizable to all but those who you'd like to know it's you.  While you are anonymous, your Riija spells grow stronger.  Requires two rainbow ferns and one firesand to cast.  

Costs: 14 mana, 1 vigor, 1 firesand, 2 rainbow ferns. Your next spell can follow a second later.

Spellpower 49 (the most is 99)
   Half your anonymity ability (99%): +49
   The moon's position: -16
   Your Riija disguise: +0
   A bonus can't go below zero: +16

## phase

SCHOOL : Riija         LEVEL : 1 

By phasing partially out of existence, one may become temporarily immune to danger... but, adventurers be warned: stay out of phase too long and you will be hurled back to the last safe place you visited.  Time spent phased out is cumulative until you reach a safe place in which to recover.  

Costs: no mana.

## morph
