# Guild chat tab's Server tab can't be clicked: the resize strip covers it

In the Modern interface the chat window's right-edge resize strip (.chat-resize) is 10 px wide and full height, and it sits over the middle of the "Server" tab, so a click on that tab starts a resize instead of selecting it. The "[Halls]" announcements are routed to the Server tab (phase 5c), so a player who goes looking for them there can't open it by clicking.

## Steps to reproduce

Log in with the Modern interface in a window about 930 px wide (the browser pane). Click the chat's "Server" tab. In the page: the tab's box is x 175-234, y 195-216; .chat-resize is x 204-214, y 191-858; document.elementsFromPoint(the tab's centre) returns [chat-resize, BUTTON, chat-tabs].

## Expected

Every tab can be clicked; the strip should stop short of the tab row, or sit outside the tabs' area.

## Actual

The Server tab's click is intercepted by the resize strip (Playwright: "<div class='chat-resize' title='Drag to resize the chat window'> intercepts pointer events", retried 20 times). The All, Chat and Combat tabs happen to be clear of it at this width. A player on a narrow window or with a wide chat may find other tabs covered, too.
