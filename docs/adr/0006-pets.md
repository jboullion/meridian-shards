# ADR 0006: Pets

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-10-12. Steps 1-3 (section 6) built 2026-10-12; step 4 (more kinds and colours) is still to do. See "As built" at the end. Builds on the relay of [ADR 0005](0005-friends.md).

## Context

We want every player of this client to be able to have one pet that follows them around, seen by everyone else with this client. Pets are purely cosmetic and client-side: the server never hears of them, and players on original clients never see them. Where a pet stands doesn't have to match between clients; *which* pet it is does.

What we have to build on:
- **Small monster art, already drawn:** the critters (`packages/world/src/ambient/critters.ts SPECIES`) draw monsters' own art small (`ViewObject.scale`), with their Kod walk cycles: rat, centipede, baby spider, spider, mutant ant, scorpion, bunny and grey bunny. Any monster's `.bgf` can be drawn the same way.
- **Objects only we see:** ambient ids (`AMBIENT_ID_BASE`, `isAmbientId`) are never in `WorldState` and never sent; art by file name through `localRes`.
- **Colours:** `EncodeTwoColorXLAT` (`townsfolk.ts`) recolours art as the server does for clothes.
- **Walking:** `navGrid.ts`, and the critters' sampled walkable spots (no grid needed).
- **The relay** of ADR 0005: who's connected, under which key and character name, per game server.
- **No levels.** Meridian 59 has none. The closest thing is the character's maximum health, which grows as they advance (stat group 1, `STAT_HP`, its `max`).

## Decisions

### 1. What a pet is

1. **One pet per character**, chosen in the Pets window (4.), kept in `localStorage` (`shards.pet.<game socket url>.<character>`): the kind, its colour, and an optional name. None at first.
2. **A pet is an ambient object** (an `AMBIENT_ID_BASE` id), drawn by `ObjectsView` like the critters. It's never in `WorldState`, never pickable, never targeted, skipped by the hover cursor, and never squished: `Critters.squish` and `squishAhead` ignore pets, and real objects always come first.
3. **The kinds** are a table in `packages/world/src/pets.ts`, like `SPECIES`: art (no corpse: pets don't die), walk cycle, scale, pace, and the health they need (2.). The first ones are the critters' species, which are already measured. Bigger monsters drawn small join later, each looked at in the game before it's added. Each kind may offer a few colours (`EncodeTwoColorXLAT`), some needing more health than the kind itself.
4. **Names** are optional, up to 16 Latin-1 characters, shown under the pet in a smaller, dimmer style than player names. Other players' pet names go through the profanity filter (`GameSession.textFilter`). An Options setting hides pet names.

### 2. Unlocking by health

1. **"Level" is maximum health.** The window reads `STAT_HP`'s maximum (not the current maximum, which spells change) and unlocks the kinds and colours up to it, in tiers, e.g. 20, 35, 50, 65, 80, 100, 120, 150. The tiers live in `pets.ts`.
2. **It's checked by our own client only.** Others can't see our health, so a modified client could show any pet. That's acceptable: it's cosmetic. Viewers draw whatever kind is announced, as long as it's in their table; an unknown kind isn't drawn.
3. Dropping below a tier (a death's health loss) keeps the pet you already have; you just can't pick it again until you're back.

### 3. Seeing other players' pets

1. **The relay keeps a registry** of the pets of everyone connected: per game server, character name → `{ kind, colour, name }`. A client sends `pet.set` when it connects and whenever the pet changes. Nothing is kept on disk; a player who isn't connected to the relay has no pet anywhere.
2. **Nobody shares where they are for this.** When player objects appear in our room (the server sends them), the client asks `pet.query` with their names, and the relay answers with the pets it knows and keeps us subscribed to those names for changes (`pet.update`) while we stay in the room. Only names we can already see are asked about, so the relay learns nothing about locations, and nobody gets a list of who uses this client beyond what the friends search already shows.
3. **Only real players get pets:** objects flagged as players, matched by name, drawn normally. A player who is invisible, in shadow form or disguised as something else (their object's drawing type or art isn't a player's) shows no pet, so a pet never gives anyone away. Their pet comes back when they do.
4. **Names are claims**, as in ADR 0005: anyone could connect calling themselves "Alice" and give her a pet. The relay keeps the first key that claimed a name while it's connected, and the client trusts a friend's pet only from that friend's key. For strangers the risk is someone else's choice of a cute animal, so we accept it.
5. **A limit:** at most 12 pets drawn, the nearest first.

### 4. The Pets window

A stone window (`kit.tsx`), from the ☰ menu (Actions → Pets…) and a Bind Editor key in the Interface tab (unbound in the original preset):
- the kinds as a grid of pictures, the locked ones dimmed with the health they need;
- the chosen one turning slowly in a preview (its art's view angles), with its colours below;
- a name box, "No pet", OK and Cancel.

And in the Bind Editor's Options tab (World box): **Show pets** (on by default), **Show pet names** (on by default). Off hides other players' pets; our own stays (choose "No pet" to hide it).

### 5. How pets move

Every client moves every pet itself, from the owner's position the server already sends. Nothing about a pet's position is ever sent.

1. **Following:** the pet heads for a spot a square behind its owner and to their right (changed 2026-10-13: every pet on the right; it was a side by the owner's name), at its kind's pace, running when the owner runs. It goes straight where the room allows (the critters' `walkable` spots), and along `findPath` when the room has a nav grid and the straight line is blocked.
2. **Idle:** when the owner stands still, the pet potters about within 0.6 of a square of its spot behind them, and rests, using a random seeded by the owner's name and the time (to the second), so clients see much the same, but nothing depends on that.
3. **Catching up:** more than 5 squares away, or the owner changed rooms, the pet fades out and fades in beside them (the critters' fades). Entering a room, it appears beside its owner.
4. **No fighting:** the pet doesn't react to combat. When its owner dies, or leaves the room, the pet fades out with them.
5. **Sounds:** now and then (every minute or two) a kind's own idle sound on the ambient bus (`GameAudio.playAmbient`) at the critters' quiet volume, with the Ambient Sounds option. Pets are silent outside populated rooms, as other ambient sounds are.
6. **Outside views and first person:** our own pet is drawn in every view; in first person you see it when you turn round.

### 6. The order to build it

1. `pets.ts` (the table, the tiers) and pets moving and drawn locally: our own pet only, no relay.
2. The Pets window.
3. The relay's registry, `pet.set`, `pet.query`, `pet.update`, and other players' pets.
4. More kinds and colours, each tried in the game first.

## Consequences

- Needs ADR 0005's relay and keys first (steps 1 and 2 there); step 1 here doesn't.
- The relay gains a small registry per connection (a few bytes each) and a query per player we see entering a room.
- Pets are ours alone: no server, Kod or protocol change, and original-client players see nothing.
- A modified client can show any pet whatever its health. Accepted: cosmetic.
- Pets add up to 12 sprites to a busy room; they share the critters' drawing path, so the cost is the same as a dozen critters.
- `AGENTS.md`'s ambient section and `docs/missing-features.md` (if anything is left out) need entries when it lands.

## As built (2026-10-12)

The pieces: `packages/world/src/pets.ts` (the kinds, the tiers, `readPetChoice`, and `Pets`, which walks every pet in a room after its owner), `tools/gateway/relay.ts` (the registry: `pet`, `petQuery`, answered with `pets`), `SocialClient.setPet`/`queryPets`, `apps/client/src/game/social.ts` (`SocialLink.setPet`, `petOf`, asking about the room's players), `pet.ts` (storage), `ui/PetsPanel.tsx`, and in `gameScene.ts` `updatePets` and `drawPetNames`. Tests: `packages/world/test/pets.test.ts`, and the registry in `tools/gateway/relay.test.ts`.

Where it differs from the decisions above:

- **Maximum health is `STAT_HP`'s `currentMax`, not its `max`** (2.1). `user.kod` sends health as `GetHealth, 0, 100, piMax_health`, so `max` is always 100 (every pet would be open to a new character) and `currentMax` is the maximum health. Spells that raise `piMax_health` raise it too; there's no other number for it.
- **A tab, not a window** (4, changed 2026-10-12): Pets is a tab beside Inventory, Spells and the rest, in the Modern character window and the Classic and phone column; N and the menu's Pets… open it. A click on a kind chooses it at once (no OK or Cancel), the name is kept as it's typed, and No pet is beside the preview.
- **One colour a kind for now** ("Natural"): recolouring monster art needs each palette translation looked at in the game first (6.4). The window's colour row shows once a kind has more.
- **The kinds** (changed 2026-10-12, after play testing): a table of their own (`PET_KINDS`), apart from the critters', from the Kod monster classes: baby spider 20, rat 30, centipede 40, fey 50, mutant ant 60, spider 70, scorpion 80, black spider (`dethspid.kod`) 90, troll 100, dragonfly 110, mollusk creature 120, Narthyl worm 130, lupogg 140, shadowbeast 150; the bunnies are gone (a saved bunny or grey bunny comes back as a rat with its name: `pets.ts RETIRED_KINDS`; add to it when a kind is taken out). Health tiers are every 10 from 20 to 150.
  - **Sizes:** small pests stay critter-sized (the rat at 0.4); rarer ones grow, the troll to half a person's height. A pet's drawn height is its art's height / shrink * scale / 64 squares; each was looked at side by side in Tos.
  - **The fey** are two kinds shown as one tile (`group`): the good fey (`faerie-g.bgf`) for karma 0 and above, the dark fey (`faerie-e.bgf`) below. Karma is the Stats group's "Karma" (-100 to 100, `user.kod SendStatKarma`); the saved choice stays as it was, and the client draws whichever the karma calls for now (`GameView ourPet`), so it changes sides as the owner does.
  - **Flying:** the fey (bobbing, `OF_BOUNCING`, as `fairy.kod` sets) and the dragonfly fly over the floor (`PetKind.fly`, the object's `z`). The dragonfly hovers standing and flies moving, its wings an overlay a frame behind and translucent, as `dfly.kod` sends them.
- **Names over heads** (1.4, changed 2026-10-12): in the players' names' style, a size smaller, not under the pet.
- **The preview turns when dragged** (4), as the paper doll's figure does, on the same dark glass.
- **The relay's messages** are `{ t: "pet", pet }` (also sent in the hello, so a reconnect restores it), `{ t: "petQuery", names }` (it replaces the names we follow; at most 60), and `{ t: "pets", pets: [{ name, key, pet }] }`, now and on each change (a null pet: none, or gone). When the first claim on a name goes, the name's pet passes to another connection claiming it.
- **A pet stuck with no way to its owner for 3 s** fades and comes back beside them, as one too far behind does.
- **Disguised or in shadow form** (3.3) is told by the owner's look having fewer than seven overlays, as the user area tells a player from anything else (`icons.ts`).
- **The Pets key** is N in the modern preset (settings version 13; not if N was taken).
