- 1705 (The Chambers of Duke Akardius [duke3.roo, RID 952]): Faction War phase 4 play-test (Duke campaign on Scout; used maintenance-port helpers for joining, renown, off-screen kills and the claim step). (1) Balance: a solo player is killed in 2-3 seconds when a battle begins. At the Valley of Ileria (twice) and the Main gate of Barloque siege, 5-8 enemy soldiers/archers/mages focused Scout (130 hp, base kit) and did 130 damage in about 3 s, before a single swing. A real player will usually be with their army, but the army spawns at the farthest point from the pole, so whoever arrives at the clash is alone with the defenders for a while. (3) Renown pacing: Soldier quest 10 + Act I 5 = 15, but Act II needs 25, so a new Soldier must find 10 renown elsewhere (battles pay 2-5, daily ground quests) before the campaign continues. Probably intended, but the Act I text doesn't say how. (4) The walker can't reach the Monk of Riija (ke1.roo, RID 2151): 'No way to object' from both teleport and kd2 arrival squares; Act II's deliver step needs it. (5) Verified working: soldier quest, Acts I-IV, battle muster/clash/result messages, a lost battle (2 renown, 250 shillings, record kept, no quest credit), a won one (quest advances, 5 renown, 1000 shillings), war death (gear broken, -1 renown, Underworld, joining the next battle ends the regroup), Champion's Gift gate (renown, then Act IV), 'march' siege (busy refusal while a battle runs, announcement, win celebrates Tos with standards, revellers, sentries and the +intellect boon message), a siege nobody leads fails after the fight timer with no penalty, and 'ledger'. Not tried: Princess and Jonas campaigns, mourning, merchant discounts, smith repair of war-broken gear, a player-led real fight.
